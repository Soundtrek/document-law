==================================================
OPERATING MODE
==================================================

First perform a COMPLETE WORKFLOW PREFLIGHT.

Do not stop at minor gaps.

Classify findings:

GREEN
already works

YELLOW
normal implementation work

ORANGE
material but safe additive change

RED
true blocker

Proceed automatically through YELLOW and ORANGE findings after documenting them.

Only stop for RED findings such as:

- destructive migration
- permission model cannot safely express required access
- identity linkage would require unsafe email merging
- company creation could accidentally grant platform Governance
- record visibility cannot be correctly enforced
- existing data would need destructive conversion

==================================================
TARGET USER JOURNEY
==================================================

Build and validate this exact loop:

1. Phil signs in with real Keycloak account.

2. Phil arrives at his real SAMMA Personal Info Center.

3. Phil creates a company.

4. Phil becomes that company's initial Owner through the normal CompanyMember / role system.

5. Phil adds or invites an employee by email.

6. SAMMA creates/preserves the correct PersonCompanyRelationship.

7. Company Owner opens the employee relationship profile.

8. Company Owner adds a Record to the employee.

9. A real synthetic test document is uploaded to persistent Garage storage.

10. Company-side view shows the record.

11. Employee signs into their own real account.

12. Employee Personal Info Center shows only the records they are authorised to see.

13. Unrelated company/private records remain invisible.

==================================================
