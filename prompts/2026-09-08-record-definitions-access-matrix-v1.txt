==================================================
CORE PRODUCT LAW — NO HARDCODE
==================================================

Business document policy must be configuration-driven.

Security invariants may be enforced in code.

Do NOT hardcode business document types such as:

- Payslip
- Employment Contract
- Proof of Address
- Medical Certificate
- Disciplinary Record

into upload routes, UI branches or permission code.

SAMMA Governance and authorised Companies must be able to define document types without a developer change.

Document access must be driven by stored policy.

Company personnel access must be role-driven.

OWNER is governance and does not automatically bypass document access policy.

==================================================
TARGET MODEL
==================================================

RecordDefinition
        ↓
RecordDefinitionVersion
        ↓
Record
        ↓
RecordFile

A definition is the document rule/template.

A record is one real document instance.

A Record must remain pinned to the exact definition version that governed it when created.

Do not silently mutate old record policy when definitions change.

7. PERSON VISIBILITY
==================================================

PERSON_TO_COMPANY + personVisible=true
→ Person continues to see their supplied document

Keep V1 binary.

No release workflow or acknowledgement logic in this task.

==================================================

- defines platform/system definitions
- can inspect system policy

- cannot grant access outside its own Company
